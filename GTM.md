# Go-to-market — Credex-style AI spend audit

## Target user

Primary: **Engineering manager or startup CTO at a 10–80 person company** who owns the corporate card for Cursor, ChatGPT Team, or GitHub Copilot Business — not “all startups,” but someone who just approved **another** \$400/mo in AI seats without a spreadsheet to justify it. Secondary: **solo founders** who expense tools personally but feel bill creep.

They don’t Google “AI cost optimizer.” They Google **“Cursor vs Copilot pricing,” “ChatGPT Team seats worth it,”** or complain in **Slack #engineering** and **X replies under Cursor’s pricing tweet.** Right before they want this tool, they’ve seen **another invoice** land or sat in a budget meeting where finance asked why AI spend doubled.

## Where they hang out

- **Subreddits:** r/startups, r/SaaS, r/webdev (weekly threads on tooling spend).
- **Slack:** Indie Hackers adjacent communities, Lenny’s Newsletter Slack (budget threads), company-internal eng Slack (you reach them via content those people forward).
- **X:** Replies to pricing announcements from Cursor, OpenAI, Anthropic; quote tweets from founders posting spend screenshots.

## First 100 users in 30 days ($0 paid ads)

1. **50 users — founder outbound:** Cold DM **30** technical founders who recently tweeted about AI tooling costs; offer to run their stack through the audit and send the **public link** only (no pitch deck). Goal: 15 replies → 10 completes → **referrals** (“send this to your CFO”).
2. **25 users — community posts:** One honest **Show HN** or **Indie Hackers** post: “I built a rule-based benchmark for AI seat spend — here’s the methodology” linking to **PRICING_DATA.md** for credibility. Respond to every comment with a personalized audit invite for people who share stack details.
3. **25 users — micro partnerships:** Ask **3** newsletter operators (engineering weekly, not giant) for a **one-line** mention in exchange for a custom paragraph they can paste — no money, mutual audience fit.

## Unfair distribution channel

**Credex’s own buyer network:** discounted AI credits already touch finance teams who **know** which companies over-bought seats. A lightweight **“forward this audit to your portfolio”** motion is credible only when the product is **neutral math**, not a sales landing page — which is why defensible rules matter.

## Week-1 traction if it works

- **200** audit completions (landing → intake → summary).
- **40** email captures (20% of completions).
- **8** public `/r/` links shared in Slack or email (measurable via self-report + optional “how did you hear” later).
- **3** booked conversations from **high-band** audits only — quality over volume.
